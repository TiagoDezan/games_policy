# Privacy Policy · Block Quest

_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

## Summary

Block Quest does not collect, sell or share any personal data with us or with third parties. It has no ads, subscriptions, accounts of its own or tracking tools. The only in-app purchase is the optional full game unlock, processed by Apple.

## What stays on the device

Level progress, stars, best scores, achievements, the powers earned and used, the gameplay statistics used to adjust difficulty, and music, sound effects and haptics preferences are stored on the iPhone or iPad itself.

## iCloud (optional)

If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress syncs between your devices through the private iCloud database (CloudKit) of your Apple Account. This data lives in your iCloud account, is handled by Apple under its privacy policy, and we cannot access it. You can turn sync off in Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

## Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your achievements, your total stars and your daily challenge scores to your Game Center account at Apple, to show achievements and leaderboards. This is managed by Apple under its privacy policy, and we do not receive this data. You can turn this off in Block Quest > Settings > Game Center, or sign out of Game Center in the device Settings.

## Full game purchase (optional)

The download is free and the first levels can be played without paying anything. If you buy the full game, the payment is made on the App Store and processed by Apple with your Apple Account, under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. The app only checks on the device itself, through Apple's purchase system (StoreKit), whether the purchase exists, so it can unlock the levels. You can use "Restore purchases" to unlock the game on another device with the same Apple Account.

## Network and third parties

The game works offline; only buying and restoring (App Store) and Game Center need a connection. We use no analytics, advertising, tracking, social login or third-party crash reporting.

## Children

The game is not specifically directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including minors.

## How to delete the data

Delete the app to remove the local data. To delete the iCloud copy, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage > Block Quest.
