# Privacy Policy · Block Quest

_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

## Summary

Block Quest does not collect, sell or share any personal data with us or with third parties. It has no ads, in-app purchases, subscriptions, accounts of its own or tracking tools.

## What stays on the device

Level progress, stars, best scores, achievements, the gameplay statistics used to adjust difficulty, and sound and haptics preferences are stored on the iPhone or iPad itself.

## iCloud (optional)

If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress syncs between your devices through the private iCloud database (CloudKit) of your Apple Account. This data lives in your iCloud account, is handled by Apple under its privacy policy, and we cannot access it. You can turn sync off in Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

## Game Center (optional)

If you use Game Center, achievements and scores are sent to Apple's Game Center to show leaderboards. This is managed by Apple, and you can sign out of Game Center at any time in the device Settings.

## Network and third parties

The game works fully offline. We use no analytics, advertising, tracking, social login or third-party crash reporting.

## Children

The game is not specifically directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including minors.

## How to delete the data

Delete the app to remove the local data. To delete the iCloud copy, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage > Block Quest.
