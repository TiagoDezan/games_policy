# Privacy Policy · Absolute Zero

_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

## Summary

Absolute Zero does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no ads, purchases, accounts or third-party tools.

## What stays on the device

The chosen code name, age range, mission progress, Bits, the answers used by the Parent Dashboard and sound preferences are stored only on the iPhone or iPad itself. This data never leaves the device and we cannot access it.

## Network and third parties

The app works offline and makes no network connections. We use no analytics, advertising, tracking, social login or third-party crash reporting.

## Children

The game is made for children aged 7 to 12 and follows the App Store Kids category rules. The Parent Area is protected by a grown-ups-only question.

## How to delete the data

In the Parent Area, tap “Erase all progress”, or delete the app from the device. Since we keep no copy, nothing is stored anywhere else.
